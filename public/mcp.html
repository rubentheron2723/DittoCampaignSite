Good technology knows its boundaries

More freedom.
Still your rules.

The exciting part is what your assistant can help you do. The important part is what stays under your control. Ditto remains the home for your recruitment data, people and processes.

◎

Your permissions travel with you.

Connected assistants act within your Ditto account's current access. Another team's private information doesn't suddenly become yours.

↗

Actions happen on your terms.

Writing requires separate authorization, normal Ditto rules, careful validation and protection against accidental repeats. No endless approval queues, no wild bulk changes.

×

You're always able to disconnect.

Choose which AI apps connect and withdraw access when you want. Linked emails and calendars can add context; Ditto doesn't send mail or create calendar events for you.

Using an external AI assistant means selected Ditto data may be shared with that provider to answer your request. Access remains subject to Ditto permissions and the provider's own policies.

01 / A day in the life

One recruiter. One client.
The entire story connects.

Follow a fictional day working with Harbourline Precision Components. Every question reveals another piece of the recruitment picture.

08:45 / Before the meeting

Start with the whole picture.

Five open jobs, two urgent follow-ups, and a salary discussion that hasn't become a decision. The assistant catches what the recruiter almost missed.

“We have three open roles, right?”
09:30 / Searching for talent

Find the right experience. Not just the right words.

Two candidates have press-shop process experience. Another has eight years in tooling, but not the experience the job actually needs.

“Who really has automotive press-shop experience?”
11:15 / Following the facts

Know what was said. And what was agreed.

Peter floated a possible increase to R75k. The job still says R65k. An intelligent assistant shouldn't quietly rewrite reality.

“Can I tell Sarah the budget's increased?”
14:20 / Working together

A conversation becomes momentum.

The notes are captured where the team works. A colleague gets context, and the follow-up belongs to the right job.

“Log the call, bring Steven in, and make sure I follow up.”
16:40 / Before signing off

Leave knowing what comes next.

The assistant connects the overdue tasks to their jobs, candidates and conversations. What needs attention is no longer buried in tabs.

“What am I forgetting before tomorrow?”
